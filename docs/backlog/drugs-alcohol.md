## Slide 1 — Oh Cannabis

_Marijuana: legalization, the rules, the industry._

- Legal since **17 October 2018**: Canada was the second country to legalize recreational cannabis nationwide, after Uruguay, and the first in the G7.
- The first legal gram went on sale at midnight in **St. John's**, where midnight arrives first; buyer Ian Power mounted it on a plaque.
- Legal age: **18** in Alberta, **21** in Quebec, 19 everywhere else. A Quebecer can buy a beer three years before a joint.
- The federal limit is **four plants** per home, but **Quebec** bans growing any; the Supreme Court upheld the ban unanimously in **2023**.
- Canopy Growth grew weed inside an abandoned **Hershey chocolate factory** in Smiths Falls, Ontario, then sold it back to Hershey in 2023 for **$53 million**.

**Images**: a cannabis plant or grow room; dried buds in a jar or plain-packaged product;
a provincial cannabis store front (SQDC, Tweed, Ontario Cannabis Store); the Smiths Falls
Hershey / Canopy factory; St. John's Water Street; a 4/20 crowd on Parliament Hill.

## Slide 2 — True north, strong and tipsy

_Mostly alcohol: history, inventions, and this year's news._

- **PEI** went dry in **1901** and stayed dry until **1948**, nearly 50 years and the longest prohibition of any province.
- **Molson**, founded in Montreal in **1786**, is the oldest brewery in North America.
- The **Caesar** was invented in Calgary in **1969**: bartender Walter Chell mixed vodka, clam and tomato juice; Canadians now drink **400 million** a year.
- Icewine grapes must freeze on the vine and be picked at **-8°C** or colder, usually at night; Canada is the world's biggest producer.
- American booze has been off most provincial shelves since **March 2025** over Trump's tariffs; Ontario still refuses to restock without a trade deal.

**Images**: a Caesar with celery and a rimmed glass; frozen grapes on the vine / a night
icewine harvest; the Molson brewery on the Montreal waterfront; a prohibition-era raid or
rum-running photo; an LCBO shelf emptied of US product (or a "Product of USA removed"
sign); a Crown Royal bag, stubby bottle, or two-four. _Difficulty: easy, except the empty
LCBO shelves (press-owned; an inbox photo would help)._

## Alternates (swap in if a fact falls flat)

- **Crown Royal** was blended by Seagram in **1939** for the first royal tour by a reigning monarch, and stayed Canada-only until 1964.
- **Free the Beer**: New Brunswick fined Gerard Comeau for driving home **14 cases** of Quebec beer; the Supreme Court sided with New Brunswick in 2018.
- Nine provinces signed a deal in **July 2026** letting you order wine, beer and spirits straight from producers in other provinces. Quebec didn't sign.
- Drinking age: **18** in Alberta, Manitoba and Quebec, 19 everywhere else.
- Roughly **78%** of cannabis spending was going to legal stores four years after legalization.
