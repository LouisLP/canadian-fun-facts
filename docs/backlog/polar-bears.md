## Check before publishing

- **870 residents / 10,000 visitors:** 870 comes from an AP story; CBC rounds to 900. The
  10,000 bear-season figure comes from Culture Trip and roughly matches Travel Manitoba's
  25,000 annual visitors, 40% of them in bear season.
- **Unlocked car doors:** widely reported custom, but one long-time resident calls the
  bear-escape reason more myth than fact. The copy states it as a custom and gives no reason beyond jumping in.
- **675-BEAR:** shown as 204-675-2327 on a town sign photo; 2327 spells BEAR. The 1969
  start date for Polar Bear Alert comes from older CBC coverage.
- **Jail:** stays range from 2 to 30 days, so the copy says "up to 30 days".
- **Two-thirds:** Polar Bears Canada (about 16,000 of 26,000). Older COSEWIC figures put
  Canada's share between 62% and 78%.
- **Safety rhyme:** a folk saying, not official advice; the copy labels it "the folk
  version".
- **Cub size:** Parks Canada's Wapusk page gives 454 to 680 g, about the size of the
  mother's paw.
