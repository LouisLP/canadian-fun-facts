# Canadian Fun Facts

A public site of weekly Canadian fun-fact slide sets, authored as files in this repo and presented from a browser instead of Google Slides.

All reader-facing copy (facts, headings, titles) follows [docs/copy-style.md](docs/copy-style.md).

## Language

**Set**:
One week's presentation: a dated, titled collection of at least one slide (three by convention) on a single topic. Identified by its slug.
_Avoid_: Deck, presentation, week

**Slide**:
One subject within a set: a heading (the hook), several facts (markdown each; 2–3 by convention), and two to four images tiled beside it.
_Avoid_: Fact, card, page

**Quiz slide**:
A slide that asks instead of tells: a question (optionally multiple choice) on its own panel, then the answer and a fact or two on the next.
_Avoid_: Question slide, trivia card

**Fact**:
One markdown nugget inside a slide — a single claim about the slide's subject.
_Avoid_: Body, bullet, point

**Topic**:
The single theme a set belongs to, chosen from a curated registry (each with a label and colour). Sets have exactly one.
_Avoid_: Category, tag, theme

**Voiceover**:
One optional audio recording per set of Louis talking through it, played from a player in the presentation. It drives the slides through its cues.
_Avoid_: Narration, audio track, commentary

**Cue**:
A timestamp in a voiceover that changes what is on screen: which panel is showing, and optionally which of its photos is lit up.
_Avoid_: Marker, keyframe, timestamp

**Panel**:
One full-screen step of a presented set: the title, each slide (a quiz slide makes two), and the thank-you. What cues and the rail count in.
_Avoid_: Section, page, screen

**Credit**:
Attribution for where a slide's image came from (name or URL). Optional per image.
_Avoid_: Attribution, courtesy

**Slug**:
A set's stable, URL-safe identity, declared in the set itself and used for permalinks. Renaming files never changes it.
_Avoid_: ID, key

**Hub**:
The site's front page: all sets in reverse-chronological order, filterable by topic.
_Avoid_: Index, home, gallery
